A Solemn Witness from 2,500 Years Ago

Why Does Every Buddhist Scripture Open with
"Thus Have I Heard"?

It was the solemn vow of Venerable Ānanda, the foremost in hearing, pledged to all future generations:

"On that day, I was sitting right there in the assembly, and I heard the Master speak these very words with my own ears..."

The Proof of Authenticity · Xuanzang's Buddhabhūmi-sūtra-śāstra (T1530)

The Fourfold Sworn Guarantee & Three Immortal Functions

The opening formula Evaṃ mayā śrutam is neither a hollow ritual nor a mythological revelation. It is an empirical deposition affirming that the teachings originate from an authentic historical Buddha.

IV

The Fourfold Depositions (Four Meanings)

Ānanda's direct accountability to history and the Sangha

  • 1. By Analogy (As Spoken by the Buddha)

    "The doctrine I report conforms perfectly to what the Tathāgata proclaimed, like a seal stamped into clay or water poured into water, without the slightest deviation."

  • 2. By Admonition (Listen in This Manner)

    "The transmitter urges future students: 'With the same utmost reverence and devotion that I received these words in the Buddha's presence, you too must listen and practice.'"

  • 3. By Inquest (Truth Under Interrogation)

    When cross-examined by the assembly—'Is this your own invention or direct transmission?'—the narrator answers: 'I heard this directly from the Master; nothing is fabricated.'

  • 4. By Commission (According to Your Trust)

    Requested by the Elders at the First Council to rehearse the Dharma, Ānanda gives his solemn assent: 'I will recount precisely what I have heard.'

III

Three Functions Sustaining the True Dharma

Delivering historical certitude across two and a half millennia

  • 1. Anti-Counterfeit Seal (Dispelling Doubt)

    Firmly confirms that the sūtra proceeds from the golden mouth of the awakened Lord, completely warding off non-Buddhist forgeries or arbitrary interpolations.

  • 2. Inspiring Joyful Faith (Faithful Practice)

    Awakening unshakeable trust in readers and practitioners, assuring them that the lineage is pure and that they may safely "accept and uphold" the teaching in daily life.

  • 3. Lineage Custodianship (Humility & Concord)

    The reciter recedes into the background, stating 'I merely heard', manifesting the sacred responsibility of transmitting without personal ego or sectarian contention.

✦ As noted by the treatise: "Placing this formula at the beginning of each sūtra demonstrates utmost gravity, preserving the Buddha's intent so that sentient beings may practice without skepticism."

Kuśinagar · Between the Twin Sāla Trees

Ānanda's Tears and the Birth of the Four Words

Beside the Hiraññavatī River, as the Buddha prepared to enter Final Nirvāṇa, Ānanda wept inconsolably. Venerable Aniruddha reminded him: 'Ask the Master: When we compile the discourses in the future, what words shall open each scripture?' The Buddha answered: 'Begin with "Thus Have I Heard".'

Śrāvastī · Jeta's Grove Monastery

Gold-Paved Grounds and the Daily Begging Round

Anāthapiṇḍika covered the earth with gold coins to purchase Prince Jeta's grove for the Sangha. Here the Buddha spent 25 rainy seasons. From the daily routine of putting on robes and collecting alms emerged the profound non-attachment of the Diamond Sūtra and the boundless bliss of the Amitābha Sūtra.
